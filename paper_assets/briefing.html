The positioning in one paragraph

The contribution is not "we LoRA-fine-tuned SAM 2" (crowded) and not "a specialist beats generalists" (expected). It is the first neuron-specialized, topology-preserving, temporally-consistent segmentation-and-tracking pipeline for live phase-contrast neuron time-lapse — a regime where foundation models fragment thin neurites and no public dataset covers phase-contrast + genuine neurons + tracking at once. Lead with that capability gap, never with the model.

02

The competitors

Two tiers — generalist foundation models, and the two task/architecture relatives you must benchmark and cite directly.

DeepSea

Cell Reports Methods 2023 · closest task relative
nearest prior art

Single-cell segmentation + tracking in 2D phase-contrast time-lapse — your exact imaging regime, and the venue you realistically target. Small U-Net + separate tracker U-Net. Seg IoU 0.90, MOTA 0.94.

You differ: neurites vs. blobs, topology loss, foundation-model transfer, prompt-based propagation. Run your model on its public dataset for an apples-to-apples number.

CellSAM

Nature Methods 2025 · closest architecture
SAM-for-cells baseline

SAM ViT-B + CellFinder bbox detector. Automatic 2D instance seg; tracking delegated downstream. Phase-contrast is one modality among many; neurons only as future work.

You differ: native temporal tracking, neurite topology, phase-contrast/neuron specialization. Cite as proof SAM transfers to microscopy.

µSAM (micro-sam)

Nature Methods 2025
generalist

Interactive + automatic microscopy segmentation with a tracking annotator — but on SAM 1, tracking = per-frame + Trackastra linking. Blob-oriented watershed head. Has seen phase-contrast (LIVECell).

You differ: native SAM 2 propagation, neurite topology losses, no watershed blob assumption.

Cellpose-SAM

bioRxiv 2025
generalist

SAM ViT-L encoder + Cellpose flow field, prompt-free, single-frame. "Superhuman" generalization on compact cells. No tracking, no topology loss.

Careful: don't claim a "round-cell bias" — cite the flow-field failure on very elongated cells (long neurons) via Omnipose instead.

SAM 2 (base)

Meta · arXiv 2408.00714
the backbone

Native video tracking via streaming memory — but zero microscopy training and no topology constraint. The paper itself admits it "struggles with very thin or fine details."

You add: domain fine-tuning + connectivity objectives on top of its memory propagation.

Topology-loss cluster

clDice · cbDice · supervoxel loss
credit, don't claim

clDice (CVPR'21), cbDice (MICCAI'24), supervoxel connectivity loss (AAAI'25) — all validated on static vessels/roads/EM/3D connectomics. None on live neuron time-lapse + a foundation model's memory.

Your whitespace: the combination and the temporal domain shift — not the individual losses.
03

Novelty ledger — be upfront

The strongest reviews come from claiming exactly the right amount. Phrase every "first" as "to our knowledge, based on a systematic search."

Genuinely novel (defensible)

  • Topology/connectivity losses applied to live neuron time-lapse, where connectivity must hold across frames as neurites extend and retract.
  • Coupled with a foundation model's temporal memory — topologically-correct mask at frame t conditions frame t+1.
  • Dual-rank LoRA+ trained through SAM 2's real video-tracking pass, so memory-attention adapters actually receive gradient.
  • Positioned to fill a real dataset gap — no public phase-contrast + neuron + tracking benchmark exists.

Not novel — credit, don't claim

  • LoRA/PEFT of SAM/SAM2 — SAM2LoRA, SAM-OCTA2, SAMed, µSAM already cover it, several on thin-vessel targets.
  • Each topology loss individually is prior art (clDice, cbDice, supervoxel loss).
  • "Foundation model for microscopy segmentation + tracking" already exists (µSAM).
  • A specialist outperforming generalists on its own niche — expected, not a headline.
