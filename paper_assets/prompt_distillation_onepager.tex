{\Large\bfseries Distilling the Prompt Pathway}\\[1pt]
{\color{acc}\rule{\textwidth}{1pt}}\\[2pt]
{\small NeuroVestigo --- closing the frame-0 conditioning gap in SAM\,2 video propagation}

\section{What}
SAM\,2 segments the first frame of a clip with the ground-truth mask supplied as a \emph{dense prompt}, and every
later frame with no prompt at all --- memory cross-attention is their only spatial context. The two regimes are
not equally good. We train the no-prompt pathway to reproduce what the prompted pathway already achieves, using
the model itself as the teacher.

\section{Why --- the measurement that motivates it}
Per-frame validation metrics, shipped 16-frame model, best epoch (110-epoch run, 5 validation recordings):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
 & frame 0 (prompted) & frames 1--15 (memory only) & gap \\
\midrule
IoU            & 0.9466 & $\sim$0.830 & $-12\%$ \\
Betti-0 error  & 0.080  & $\sim$0.70  & $\mathbf{\sim 9\times}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The topology gap is an order of magnitude larger than the region gap: losing the prompt does not blur the mask,
it \textbf{fragments neurites}. And frames 1$\to$15 drift only \textbf{0.017 in total}, so this is a \emph{single
conditioning step}, not accumulating propagation error --- there are two regimes, prompted and not, and every
unprompted frame sits at the same level. Improving that pathway lifts all fifteen frames together.

The decoder can produce a 0.947 mask of these images. It cannot reach that without a spatial prior.

\section{Definitions}
Let $f_\theta$ be the network, $x_t$ frame $t$, $m_t$ its ground-truth mask, $M_{<t}$ the memory bank, $\pi$ the
dense prompt. Teacher and student are the \textbf{same weights on the same image}, differing only in $\pi$:
\[
\underbrace{\tilde{y}_t = f_\theta\!\left(x_t,\ \pi = m_t\right)}_{\text{teacher: privileged}}
\qquad\qquad
\underbrace{\hat{y}_t = f_\theta\!\left(x_t,\ \pi = \varnothing,\ M_{<t}\right)}_{\text{student: inference-time}}
\]
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}
= \lambda_1\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{Dice}}\!\left(\hat{y}_t,\ \sg\!\left[\sigma(\tilde{y}_t)\right]\right)
+ \lambda_2\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{cbDice}}\!\left(\hat{y}_t,\ \sg\!\left[\sigma(\tilde{y}_t)\right]\right)
\]
$\sg[\cdot]$ is the stop-gradient. The Dice target is \emph{soft}, $\sigma(\tilde y_t)\in[0,1]$: it encodes which
pixels the model finds genuinely ambiguous, which a binary mask cannot. Prompt dropout withholds frame 0's prompt
with probability $p_e = p\cdot\min(1, e/E_w)$, with $p=0.4$ and $E_w=40$.

\textbf{Where the two mechanisms meet.} Which frames are distilled depends on whether the prompt was dropped:
\[
\mathcal{D} \;=\;
\underbrace{\{1\}}_{\text{always}}
\;\cup\;
\underbrace{\{0\ \text{iff}\ \pi_0=\varnothing\}}_{\text{only when dropped}}
\;\cup\;
\underbrace{\text{sample}(k,\ \{2,\dots,T-1\})}_{k=4}
\]
Frame 0 is excluded whenever it keeps its prompt, because then student and teacher receive the same input and
$\hat y_0 = \tilde y_0$ exactly --- the term is identically zero and the decoder pass is wasted. Dropout is what
puts frame 0 into the student's regime in the first place, and therefore what makes it worth a teacher at all.
This is the only place the two additions interact.

\section{The gradient, and why $\lambda$ decides the outcome}
Differentiating through the stop-gradient leaves \textbf{one} path --- the student's:
\[
\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}
= \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}}{\partial \hat{y}_t}\cdot\frac{\partial \hat{y}_t}{\partial \theta},
\qquad\text{no term through } \tilde y_t .
\]
But $\theta$ is \emph{shared}, so the target $\sg[\sigma(f_\theta(x,m))]$ is itself a function of the $\theta$
being updated. Two consequences.

\textbf{(i) It is not minimising a function.} With the stop-gradient the update is not $\nabla$ of any scalar in
$\theta$: this is a dynamical system, not an optimisation problem, with no energy descended and no convergence
guarantee. (Structurally the situation of BYOL / SimSiam, which need an EMA or predictor to stay stable.)

\textbf{(ii) $\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}$ has a floor above zero.} Reaching $0$ would need
$f_\theta(x,\varnothing,M) = f_\theta(x,m)$ for one $\theta$ --- impossible, because $m$ carries information absent
from $x$. So $\nabla_\theta\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}} \neq 0$ \emph{always}, and it pushes $\theta$ forever. Writing
the full objective with $\alpha$ for the distillation weight:
\[
\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\text{GT}}+\alpha\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\text{GT}}(\theta^\star)=-\alpha\,\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}(\theta^\star)\neq 0,
\qquad
\boxed{\;\theta^\star-\theta_{\text{GT}} \approx -\alpha\,H^{-1}\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}\;}
\]
with $H$ the Hessian of $\mathcal{L}_{\text{GT}}$. \textbf{You converge somewhere that is not the ground-truth
optimum, displaced in proportion to $\alpha$.} That is not a flaw to remove but a dial to set: part of
$\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}}$ points toward shapes the architecture can actually represent (useful
regularisation), and part points at the unreachable residual (bias, linear in $\alpha$). Small $\alpha$: benefit
dominates. Large $\alpha$: bias dominates.

\end{center}

Result: \textbf{val IoU 0.8340} against a \textbf{0.8377} baseline, then flat for 44 epochs --- the signature of
converging to the optimum of the wrong objective, exactly as the boxed equation predicts.

\textbf{Why frame 1 and not any other.} The memory bank is causal: frame 1's prediction enters $M_{<t}$ for every
later $t$, so $\partial\mathcal{L}(t)/\partial(\text{quality at frame }1)\neq 0$ for all $t>1$, while frame 12
influences only three frames. Frame 1 has the largest downstream influence in the clip.

\section{Cost and how it will be judged}
No new parameters: 5{,}557{,}250 trainable of 39{,}876{,}930 (13.9\%), unchanged. The image encoder is shared, so
each teacher frame costs one prompt-encoder $+$ mask-decoder pass. Net compute is \emph{below} run 1:
$5\times70 = 350$ teacher passes against $4\times110 = 440$.

\textbf{Read Betti-0 before IoU} --- the mechanism predicts topology moves first, since that is where the $9\times$
gap is. And because distillation is off until epoch 40, the run carries its own control: epochs 1--39 are ordinary
training, so a bend in the curve \emph{at that boundary} is the effect. \emph{Status: run 2 in progress.}
